Inspired by the dark luxury aesthetic of 21st.dev's Clandestine template by Maxim Bortnikov, **Clandestine Atelier** is an ultra-exclusive digital portal for private aesthetics medical practices and luxury wellness sanctuaries.

---

## 1. Key Highlights
- **Editorial Luxury Dark Aesthetic**: Deep carbon obsidian, champagne gold typography, and glassmorphic cards.
- **Private Suite Reservation Engine**: Integrated calendar booking and practitioner selection.
- **ScaleNova Master Lead Ingestion**: Synchronized via Google Apps Script endpoint to `Demo 7 - Clandestine`.

## 2. Integrated Pages
1. `index.html`: Sanctuary hero, curated protocols, physician roster, and VIP private society.
2. `services.html`: Full clinical treatments directory with pricing and duration.
3. `booking.html`: Interactive multi-field private suite reservation flow.
4. `about.html`: Philosophy of discretion and architectural privacy.
5. `contact.html`: Private concierge and location directory.
6. `terms.html` & `privacy.html`: Medical confidentiality compliance.
